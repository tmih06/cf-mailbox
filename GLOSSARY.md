# Personal Unified Mailbox

A personal email workspace that brings its owner's existing mailboxes together. Each person hosts their own independent copy rather than joining a shared public service.

## Language

**Owner**:
The person who hosts and uses a personal copy of the application.
_Avoid_: Tenant, customer

**Connected mailbox**:
An existing email mailbox owned by the owner and connected to the application.
_Avoid_: User account

**Unified mailbox**:
The owner's combined workspace for their connected mailboxes; it is not itself a replacement email provider.

**Personal instance**:
An independently hosted copy of the application for one owner.
_Avoid_: Shared service
